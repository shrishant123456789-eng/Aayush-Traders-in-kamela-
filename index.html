Aayush Traders is a premier hardware, paint, tiles and sanitary store located at Kamela Chauraha, in front of Kamela Gate, Haidergarh, Barabanki, Uttar Pradesh. We have been serving the local community, contractors, and builders with genuine products at competitive prices.

Our store stocks a vast range of construction materials — from PVC pipes and plumbing accessories to premium paints, decorative tiles, and complete bathroom sanitary solutions. Whether you're building a home or renovating a commercial space, we are your one-stop destination.
